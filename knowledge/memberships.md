## What We Do

GetRightCover provides digital services relating to traffic challan management and vehicle insurance-related services.

## Traffic Challan Services

Customers can fetch challans for a registered vehicle and search challan records by vehicle registration number, challan number, or offence. The platform provides challan details and a facility to request a challan copy.

Customers can view returned challan records, including pending items, for vehicles associated with their account. Available details include offence, amount, date, and status.

Online payment is available for eligible challan payment or settlement flows. Depending on the flow, the portal may offer Razorpay checkout or wallet payment. A GetRightCover settlement offer should not be described as the same thing as a direct government fine payment unless the specific flow confirms this.

Customers can view challan and payment statuses in the portal. A receipt can be downloaded when one has been generated and is available.

The platform does not guarantee that every challan issued by every authority will appear. Do not promise dispute outcomes, challan cancellation, waivers, or resolution timelines unless confirmed by an approved policy or system.
